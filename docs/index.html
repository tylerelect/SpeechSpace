Ready for liftoff.

SpeechSpace asks one question each day: what was the best part? Tell your story out loud, and it lands on your calendar, in your story list, and on a star map you can connect.

Try the demo See how it works

One mission a day

No typing needed. One question, one big button, and a story worth keeping.

  1. Tell your story

    Press "Tell my story" and say what the best part of your day was, for up to 90 seconds.

  2. Get story helpers

    Your words become text. Gemini Flash can suggest a short highlight, your favorite thing, and a few tags.

  3. Save it to your sky

    Check the suggestions, change anything you like, and save. Your story shows up on that day.

Connect your stories like stars

The star map lays out every story as a card you can drag, zoom, and link. Join the kite day to the castle day under "Family activities" and watch the constellation grow.

Tap "New ideas" for suggested idea stars, like trying another outdoors adventure. Keep the ones you like and dismiss the rest.

See your month at a glance

My days shows which days have stories, how many active days you've had, and the milestones a parent has marked.

  • Switch between day, week, month, and year
  • Tap any date to read what happened
  • Add a story for a day you missed

Every story, ready to revisit

Stories from before keeps each one with its date, a one-line highlight, and tags like Outdoors, Family, or Favorites, so it's easy to find the big blue kite again months later.

Grown-ups approve every recording

A guardian checks "approved" before a recording is sent. Kids can also type what happened, name their story, and add their favorite thing.

A space for everyone at home

Each person gets their own profile and theme. Kids profiles get playful missions and a space look. Adult profiles get a calmer one.

Parents stay in charge

Audio isn't kept. Recordings are sent for transcription and then thrown away. They're never stored in our database.

You approve what's saved. AI story helpers stick to plain facts: a highlight, a favorite thing your child named, and a few tags. Nothing is saved until you review it.

It's a journal, not a test. SpeechSpace doesn't score, diagnose, or judge anyone's development.

SpeechSpace is an early prototype built at Hack The Nest. Please try it with demo profiles until parent accounts and consent controls are in place.
