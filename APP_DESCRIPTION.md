# SpeechSpace

**Little stories. Stronger connections.**

## One-sentence description

SpeechSpace is a guided journaling app that helps children share their day and
gives busy parents a starting point for meaningful conversations.

## App description

Family life moves quickly, and the question “How was your day?” does not always
lead to more than “Fine.” SpeechSpace helps children find the words to tell
their stories through a playful, space-inspired journal with voice and text
entry, gentle prompts, and optional AI-assisted follow-up suggestions.

A child can begin with a moment from school, something they learned, or a
favorite part of the day. Prompts encourage them to add details: who was there,
what happened next, and what made the moment memorable. Parents can review the
entries, revisit them on a calendar, and explore connections between memories.

Designed for families balancing work, school, and everyday responsibilities,
SpeechSpace makes it easier to capture the little moments that might otherwise
get lost in a busy week. Those stories give parents something specific to ask
about and children something they are excited to share. It supports an ongoing
family conversation, with parents involved in reviewing and responding to what
their children record.

## Short presentation pitch

“Sometimes the hardest part of connecting after a busy day is knowing where to
start. SpeechSpace gives children a playful place to share their day in their
own words, with prompts that help them tell a fuller story. Parents can revisit
those moments and use them to start more meaningful conversations. We help busy
families turn everyday stories into opportunities to connect.”

## The problem we address

Parents want to stay involved, but busy schedules can make daily check-ins feel
rushed. Children may also need help remembering their day or expressing what
they want to share. SpeechSpace reduces the effort of getting a conversation
started and keeps those stories available to revisit together.

## Current prototype features

- Separate profiles with child-friendly themes.
- Voice recording and text entry, with transcription when the backend is configured.
- Writing prompts and optional AI-generated suggestions based on an entry.
- Editable memories with highlights, favorites, tags, and parent-selected milestones.
- Calendar views, a memory timeline, an activity heat map, and a mind map linking related memories.

## Positioning for the team

Describe the audience as **busy parents who want an easier way to stay connected
with their children**. Emphasize support, curiosity, and shared memories.

Keep claims grounded in the prototype: follow-up suggestions appear in the entry
editor; this is not yet an autonomous voice interviewer. The activity heat map
shows recorded journal activity, not a child's wellbeing or developmental progress.
The app does not diagnose, monitor school performance, or replace a parent's
attention. Presentations should use synthetic test data with the current prototype.
